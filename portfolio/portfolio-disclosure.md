# Portfolio Scope and Attribution

This repository documents hands-on learning and practice with LLM evaluation methods. It includes my product-oriented framing, learning notes, and analysis alongside third-party educational assets and supplied datasets.

Dataset files in `datasets/` are preserved without content changes. Original license and attribution notices for third-party assets remain in place. My contribution should be understood as the additional portfolio documentation, framing, and any separately marked experiments or analysis—not as authorship of every included template, example, or dataset.
